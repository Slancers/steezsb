# WallRide Park Brand Guidelines

Updated July 23, 2026.

## Direction

WallRide is Hyderabad's action-sports home. The visual identity is light, premium, energetic and community-rooted. STEEZ is a motion reference, not a visual template. WallRide should feel cleaner, more spacious and more polished.

Design dials:

- Design variance: 7/10
- Motion intensity: 5/10
- Visual density: 4/10

## Color system

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary canvas | Paper | `#F7F7F4` | Page background and navigation |
| Secondary canvas | Cool grey | `#ECECE8` | Alternate light sections and footer |
| Primary text | Ink | `#17171B` | Headlines, body and borders |
| Brand accent | WallRide purple | `#7138D3` | CTAs, links, active states and graphics |
| Brand tint | Soft purple | `#E8E0F5` | Light feature sections |
| Graphic accent | Acid lime | `#D8FF57` | Kinetic highlights and small motion moments |

Purple is the only conversion color. Acid lime is a supporting graphic accent and should not compete with primary CTAs.

Avoid dark page backgrounds, neon glows, purple gradients and pure black or pure white.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display and hero | Barlow Condensed | 900 | Uppercase, compressed and short-lined |
| Section headings | Barlow Condensed | 800-900 | Editorial sports-poster rhythm |
| Body and UI | Manrope | 400-700 | Clear, premium and readable |
| Labels | Manrope | 800 | Uppercase with restrained tracking |

Avoid decorative section numbering, repeated micro-labels and oversized type that creates awkward mobile breaks.

## Logo

The approved logo is the black WallRide Park mark.

- Source: `Isolated wrp logo - SVG.svg`
- Production asset: `public/wallride/logo-mark.png`
- Use black on paper, cool grey, soft purple and acid lime.
- A white version is allowed only over photography or dark media.
- Never stretch, recolor purple, add a drop shadow, or place inside a decorative badge.
- Preserve clear space equal to roughly one letter width around the mark.

The source SVG is a complex traced asset. The production mark is a lightweight transparent derivative for reliable web rendering.

## Layout and components

- Maximum content width: 1280px.
- Navigation height: 72px maximum.
- Prefer spacing, strong typography and sparse dividers over generic cards.
- Corners use a consistent 3px radius.
- Buttons are compact rectangular controls, not pills.
- Use one marquee maximum per page.
- Full-width photography is reserved for the homepage hero or major storytelling moments.
- Internal page heroes combine editorial type with a restrained line illustration or one photograph.

## Photography

Photography should feel real, active and community-led. Prioritize riders in motion, coaching, first attempts, wide park context and community events.

The current social-media images are temporary. Replace them with clean landscape photography when the final shoot arrives. Homepage hero target: at least 2400 x 1350 pixels.

## Motion language

Motion is restrained and polished.

Approved motion:

- 320-450ms route entrances using opacity and a small vertical shift
- 600-750ms scroll reveals for major headings and content groups
- One slow horizontal marquee per page when it carries meaningful claims
- Short SVG line-drawing sequences that explain riding or park movement
- Subtle 2-3% image scale on hover
- Physical button press feedback

Avoid scroll hijacking, constant parallax, cursor-following effects, multiple marquees and large perpetual loops. Animate only transform, opacity and SVG stroke offset. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Voice

WallRide sounds direct, welcoming and knowledgeable: concrete over poetic, confident without bravado, friendly to first-timers, credible to experienced riders, and community-led rather than corporate.

## Accessibility and performance

- WCAG AA contrast minimum
- Visible keyboard focus
- Descriptive image alt text
- One-column mobile fallbacks
- Hero content and CTA visible in the initial viewport
- Reserved image space to prevent layout shift
- Optimized production imagery and logo assets
