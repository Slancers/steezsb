# WallRide Park Brand Guidelines

Source: WallRide discovery materials, finalized July 22, 2026.

## Direction

Polished, premium, and community-rooted. WallRide should feel like an international action-sports destination with a strong Hyderabad scene. It is not corporate and not performatively street.

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purple | `#A855F7` | Hero backgrounds, CTAs, claims strips, accent bands |
| Near-black | `#09090B` | Navigation, footer, dark sections, text |
| Deep purple | `#3B0764` | Dark purple sections and overlays |
| Purple tint | `#F5F3FF` | Cards, information blocks, subtle backgrounds |
| Off-white | `#FAFAFA` | Content sections |

Logo rule: use the black logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark or purple backgrounds. Never use a purple logo.

## Typography

- Display and hero headings: Syne, 700–800.
- Body, labels, navigation, and UI: Inter, 400–600.
- Labels: uppercase, 12–14px, with generous letter spacing.
- Hero headings: approximately 40–72px depending on viewport.
- Body copy: 15–16px with approximately 1.7 line height.

## Layout and UI

- Maximum content width: 1200px.
- Section padding: approximately 80px desktop and 48px mobile.
- Cards: 12px radius.
- Buttons: pill-shaped, 9999px radius.
- Primary CTA: purple background with near-black text.
- Secondary CTA: transparent with purple border and text.
- Call button: white background with near-black text.

Use bold type, clean layouts, and park photography as the main visual material. Preserve generous space and clear conversion paths.

## Voice

Confident, welcoming, direct, and specific. Speak to riders, parents, beginners, progressing skaters, BMX riders, and the wider Hyderabad community.
