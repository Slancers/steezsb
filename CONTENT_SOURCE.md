## About

### Built because Hyderabad needed a place to ride

Before WallRide, riders in Hyderabad had the passion but not the infrastructure. Hamza saw skate culture growing across India while Hyderabad had no park, started WallRide as a personal passion project, then took it fully professional.

In 2017, that idea became WallRide Park: a safe and engaging environment supporting alternative culture. The park combines an asphalt pump track built by Velosolutions with skate terrain designed for progression.

### More than a park

WallRide exists to give action sports a real home in Hyderabad: a place where beginners feel welcome, experienced riders can progress, and the next generation can see BMX and skateboarding as more than a hobby.

Closing line: Ride. Learn. Progress. Together.
